# Stump the Machine (archive)

Riddlen's second design ran on the Amoy testnet in September 2026: people wrote riddles, a panel
of AI models tried each one before anyone could play, and a riddle paid only if the machines had
failed. It was retired the same month in favour of the hunt, which moves the difficulty out of
the text and into the world. These pages are kept as they were.
